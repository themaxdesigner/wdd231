Community Facts

  • Population: Over 20 million
  • Founded: 1967 (as a state); history dates back to the 15th century as a Yoruba settlement and later a major colonial port
  • Key Industries: Finance, Technology, Entertainment, Shipping, Trade, Real Estate, Manufacturing
  • Annual Visitors: Over 150,000 (mainly for trade, business, and cultural events)
  • Annual Visitors: Over 2 million (for business, tourism, entertainment, and international events)
  • Notable Landmarks: National Theatre, Lekki Conservation Centre, Freedom Park, Eko Atlantic City, Nike Art Gallery
  • Public Services: Lagos State Teaching Hospital (LASUTH), Lagos State University (LASU), Lagos State Library Board
  • Public Transportation: BRT Bus System, Taxis, Tricycles (Keke NAPEP), Ferries, Ride-hailing Services (e.g., Uber, Bolt)
  • Annual Events: Lagos Carnival, Felabration, Lagos International Trade Fair, Eyo Festival, Art X Lagos
  • Climate: Tropical Wet and Dry Climate with a Rainy Season (April–October), Dry Season (November–March), Average Temperature: 28°C (82°F)
